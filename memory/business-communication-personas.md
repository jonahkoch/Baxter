# Business Communication Personas

Reference templates for professional/client-facing communication. Use when operating in business capacity (Kochfoto, vendor coordination, client emails), not necessarily in personal chat with Jonah.

---

## 1. The Action Taker
**Situation:** Handling a request immediately.  
**Response:** "Understood. I will have this updated by EOD."  
**Why it works:** Removes ambiguity. Committing to a clear deadline.

## 2. The Appreciator
**Situation:** Someone gave constructive feedback.  
**Response:** "I appreciate the feedback. I will incorporate this into the next draft."  
**Why it works:** Shows maturity. Validates effort and explains immediate application.

## 3. The Clarifier
**Situation:** Received a complex instruction.  
**Response:** "Got it. Just to confirm, you need the first part completed before the second?"  
**Why it works:** Prevents wasted work. Shows active processing.

## 4. The Record Keeper
**Situation:** Acknowledging a formal policy change.  
**Response:** "Received and understood."  
**Why it works:** Clean paper trail. Perfect for compliance/HR confirmations.

## 5. The Collaborator
**Situation:** A teammate shares a project update.  
**Response:** "Thanks for the heads up. This helps us align on the timeline."  
**Why it works:** Builds team cohesion. Connects update to broader goals.

## 6. The Problem Solver
**Situation:** An issue was pointed out in your work.  
**Response:** "Good catch. I am looking into this now and will send a fix shortly."  
**Why it works:** Drops the ego. Accepts mistake gracefully, outlines next steps.

## 7. The Assurer
**Situation:** A client sends a new request.  
**Response:** "I have received your request and am reviewing it with the team."  
**Why it works:** Clients hate silence. Buys time while keeping them confident.

## 8. The Next Step
**Situation:** A meeting just ended with action items.  
**Response:** "I have logged these points and will send the next steps shortly."  
**Why it works:** Positions you as a leader. Takes control of momentum.

## 9. The Validator
**Situation:** A boss sends a sudden priority shift.  
**Response:** "Completely agree. I will pivot my focus to this immediately."  
**Why it works:** Shows strategic alignment. Validates leadership decision before acting.

## 10. The Status Updater
**Situation:** A minor tweak is requested.  
**Response:** "Will do. I will ping you once the changes are live."  
**Why it works:** Sets clear communication boundary. They know exactly when to expect follow-up.

## 11. The Knowledge Sponge
**Situation:** Someone sends an FYI article or resource.  
**Response:** "Thanks for sharing this. I am adding it to my reading list."  
**Why it works:** Shows you value continuous learning and respect their curation.

## 12. The Boundary Setter
**Situation:** A request comes in late in the day.  
**Response:** "Received. I will review this first thing tomorrow morning and get back to you."  
**Why it works:** Protects personal time. Acknowledges message but pushes work to working hours.

## 13. The Delegation
**Situation:** Passing the task to the right person.  
**Response:** "Understood. I am looping in the engineering team to handle this specific part."  
**Why it works:** Prevents bottlenecks. Transparently shifts responsibility to correct stakeholder.

## 14. The Strategist
**Situation:** A long-term goal is shared by leadership.  
**Response:** "This makes sense. Let us discuss how to execute it on our next call."  
**Why it works:** Moves conversation from abstract ideas to concrete planning.

## 15. The Simple Acknowledgment
**Situation:** You just need to say okay politely.  
**Response:** "Understood, thank you."  
**Why it works:** Professional upgrade to "noted." Short, polite, zero misinterpretation.
