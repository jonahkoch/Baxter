## What `info_action` Actually Is

Info actions are **advisory only** — they don't change protocol parameters, move treasury funds, or trigger hard forks. They're essentially on-chain polls to gauge community sentiment on a topic. Think of them as "the ecosystem is asking: what do we think about X?"

All three governance bodies vote, but since nothing enacts automatically, the threshold is more about demonstrating broad consensus than technical requirements.
