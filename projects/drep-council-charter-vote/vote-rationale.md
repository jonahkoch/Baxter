# DRep Council Interim Charter v0.2 — Vote Rationale

**Charter Version:** v0.2 (15 September 2026)  
**Organization:** [DRep Council](https://drepcouncil.org) · [@DRepCouncil](https://x.com/DRepCouncil)

---

I vote YES to ratify the DRep Council Interim Charter v0.2. This is an excellent foundational framework that balances structure and flexibility for a launch-phase organization. It correctly positions the Council as a coordination and standards-setting body rather than a funding authority or voting bloc, and embeds strong anti-capture and transparency principles from day one.

I submit two dissenting views for the record. These do not change my vote, but represent gaps to address in Charter v1 or Council procedure.

---

## Dissenting View 1 — Extend Voting Window from 7 to 15–30 Days

Section 8.6 establishes a 7-day voting period. This is insufficient for meaningful deliberation on substantive matters. The final draft is the only document members can truly judge; relying on pre-submission consultation introduces verification risk. Council members also hold responsibilities to employment, family, and their DRep constituencies — a 7-day window forces rushed judgment on ecosystem-wide decisions. Good governance requires time for ingestion, analysis, and discussion. For urgent matters, the Council could use a lower-stakes signaling pool or straw poll instead.

---

## Dissenting View 2 — Explicitly Include DRep Onboarding in Scope

Section 6.1 lists the Council's 2026 scope. DRep onboarding and member acquisition should be explicitly added, not treated as an implicit byproduct. Those who found the Council organically cannot assume others will be equally motivated — passive discovery is not a growth strategy. The Council's legitimacy scales with participation; a small group risks becoming an echo chamber. We should become a pragmatic, nimble KOL for the ecosystem — a macro-oracle that synthesizes information across Cardano. AG/OG success represents high-visibility coordination that can demonstrate value to prospective members. Without an explicit membership-growth mandate, this critical work will be deprioritized.

The charter states the Council is "a body that can produce public recommendations, rationales, feedback, standards, and reports." I would add: "and actively recruit, onboard, and support new DRep members to expand the Council's representativeness and impact."

---

The Interim Charter v0.2 is a strong enough foundation to begin operations. My YES vote reflects confidence in the document's core architecture: the anti-capture provisions, the log₁₀ voting model, the clear delineation of what the Council is and is not, and the commitment to review and replace this charter by year-end.

My dissenting views are offered constructively. I hope they will inform the Charter v1 drafting process and interim Council procedures as we learn by doing.
