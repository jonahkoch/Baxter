## Key v17 Additions Over v15

### Three Unified Scorecards (100 points each)
1. **Commercial, Hybrid and Infrastructure Scorecard** — 12 categories
2. **Public-Good and Civic-Service Scorecard** — 12 categories  
3. **Marketing and Adoption Scorecard** — 12 categories

### Eight Specialist Public-Good Modules
- Constitutional / Governance work
- Independent research
- Education / Curriculum
- Standards stewardship
- Community coordination
- Translation / Accessibility
- Security monitoring / Emergency response
- Recurring maintenance of genuine public good

### Five Forms of Public Return
1. Public asset (code, standards, research, data)
2. Public service (monitoring, maintenance, education)
3. Institutional capacity (skills, processes, stewardship)
4. Public learning (findings, pilots, negative results)
5. Avoided loss (reduced security/operational risk)

### Mixed-Proposal Rule
- Separate material workstreams, budgets, milestones, risks, rights
- Score each under matching scorecard
- Each must pass its own hard gates
- Do not average failed into passing
- Commercial upside cannot hide in public-good labels

### 32 Treasury-Hard Rules (Part III, Section 25)
v17 adds 3 rules over v16's 29:
- New rules on productive circulation, subsidy-loop detection, and Cardano-native duplication

### Constitutional Preflight — Mandatory 7-Field Record
1. Constitution and guardrails (canonical source, version/hash, dates)
2. Governance-action content (ID, type, submission, rationale, immutable links)
3. Self-contained withdrawal (all commitments incorporated or immutably referenced)
4. NCL and capacity (active limit, period, withdrawals counted, remaining capacity)
5. Administrator and oversight (named roles, authority, independence, reporting, audit, replacement)
6. Custody and disbursement (receiving addresses, segregation, control, tranche logic)
7. Dispute and recovery (agreement, path, refund, clawback, replacement, unspent-funds rules)
8. Voting and enactment state (bodies, thresholds, deadlines, expiry, CC state, conditions)

### Dynamic-Law Warning
- Must verify current Constitution, guardrails, NCL, governance rules at time of voting
- Do not rely on archived guidance when newer enacted rules exist
- Where sources conflict, record conflict, identify governing source, explain reliability effect

### Rule Hierarchy
1. On-chain ledger rules and enacted guardrails prevail over this framework
2. Current enacted Constitution prevails over archived explainers
3. Treasury Withdrawal and immutable references prevail over marketing pages or informal promises
4. Binding contracts and executable controls carry more weight than non-binding intentions

### Request-Size Classification (5 bands)
| Size | Nominal ADA | Share of Active NCL | Share of Category |
|------|-------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Small | 0–250K | <0.25% | <2% |
| Medium | 250K–1M | 0.25%–<1% | 2%–<5% |
| Large | 1M–4M | 1%–<3% | 5%–<15% |
| Very large | 4M–20M | 3%–<7.5% | 15%–<30% |
| Systemic | >20M | ≥7.5% | ≥30% |

**Classification rules:**
- Use highest category triggered by any test
- Aggregate linked tranches, affiliates, related parties, parallel requests
- Move UP one band for: concentrated custody, full upfront withdrawal, unproven administrator, weak recovery rights, category dominance
- Do NOT move down because purpose is valuable

### Score Override Discipline
- High score but wrong priority/instrument → No or defer
- High score but excessive opportunity cost/concentration → No
- Passing score but failed hard gate → No
- Small staged public experiment with high uncertainty → May be Yes if frontier lane clears
- Low confidence due to applicant omission → No
- Low reliability due to external uncertainty/conflict → Abstain
- Low growth but severe maintenance/security harm if unfunded → Use avoided-loss analysis

### Fast Workflow
1. Verify current constitutional and governance-action requirements
2. Run funding-architecture screen before scoring
3. Classify proposal, select scorecard, aggregate linked exposure
4. Apply universal hard gates and relevant specialist module
5. Score from zero, apply request-size thresholds and category minimums
6. Run decision-reliability test, map to Yes/No/Abstain
7. Publish short rationale, preserve score sheet
8. After funding, update outcomes ledger

### Funding Architecture Screen (Before Scoring)
Step 1: Priority fit — Is this a defensible Treasury priority?
Step 2: Instrument fit — Is the requested instrument appropriate?
Step 3: Exposure discipline — Does the size match the priority and risk?
