This Treasury Withdrawal funds Dano Finance to accelerate the **DeFi Kernel** as an open standard for shared liquidity, on-chain financial intents, and global order-book coordination on Cardano. The request is **3,333,000 ADA** (3.3M delivery + 33K admin fee to Minswap Labs).

## Work Packages

| WP | Budget | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| WP1 – DeFi Kernel Registry | 300K ADA | Public registry, submission process, compatibility docs |
| WP2 – Spot Leverage Order Book | 1M ADA | Leveraged spot trading protocol |
| WP3 – American Options Protocol | 1M ADA | Options market-making pools |
| WP4 – Composable DeFi Transaction Builder SDK | 1M ADA | Developer SDK for discovery and composition |

## Key KPIs

- **Spot Leverage:** $1M rolling 30-day trading volume within 90 days of mainnet
- **American Options:** $1M rolling 30-day notional volume within 90 days of mainnet
- **SDK:** At least 1 external wallet/bot/indexer integration
- **Registry:** 2+ DeFi Kernel-compatible contracts documented
- **Revenue share:** 5% of protocol fees returned to Treasury for 12 months

## Team Track Record

Dano Finance currently operates with approximately **$18M TVL** and **10,000+ on-chain transactions** across lending, concentrated liquidity, oracle aggregation, and borrowing. The team includes fallen-icarus, author of the DeFi Kernel concept and cardano-swaps.

## Why This Matters

The DeFi Kernel addresses a genuine structural gap in Cardano DeFi: fragmented liquidity silos, inconsistent contract metadata, and custom integration work for every protocol. By defining a common standard for permissionless, composable, discoverable contracts, it enables the global orderbook pattern that Dano has already demonstrated working in production.

The registry and SDK are particularly valuable as **public infrastructure** — open, fee-free, royalty-free standards that any builder can adopt without licensing costs.
