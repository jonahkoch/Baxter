# Assessment and Vote Rationale: NCL Increase (350M to 500M ADA)

## Proposal Overview

This InfoAction proposes raising the Net Change Limit for Treasury Withdrawals from 350 million ADA to 500 million ADA for the period covering Epochs 613 through 713. The NCL functions as a fixed cap on total ADA removed from the Treasury through enacted withdrawals. It does not authorize any specific spending. Each Treasury Withdrawal still requires separate DRep approval at 67 percent threshold.

## Current Voting Status

As of Epoch 646 this proposal has 30.87 percent Yes votes and 69.13 percent No votes. It requires 50 percent Yes to pass under TREASURY-01a. It expires at Epoch 647.

## Detailed Assessment

### What the NCL Actually Does

The Net Change Limit is a fiscal guardrail not a spending mandate. It caps cumulative Treasury outflows over a defined period. When the original 350 million ADA limit was agreed around Epoch 613 in February 2026 ADA traded at approximately 30 cents. Today proposals reference approximately 16 cents per ADA. This represents a 47 percent decline in the US dollar value of the same ADA amount.

### The Purchasing Power Problem

The core issue that has not been adequately addressed in public debate is purchasing power erosion. The original 350 million ADA limit represented roughly 105 million US dollars in fiscal capacity. At current prices that same 350 million ADA equals roughly 56 million US dollars. This is not a tightening that DReps voted for. It is an accidental compression caused by external price action.

Projects budget in US dollars. The NCL caps ADA outflows. When ADA drops the same deliverable scope requires more ADA to maintain equivalent value. Proposers are not being greedy when they ask for larger ADA amounts. They are preserving scope against a falling currency.

### Why 500M Represents Partial Restoration Not Expansion

A fully purchasing power adjusted NCL would require approximately 650 million ADA to restore the original 105 million US dollar equivalent at current prices. The proposed 500 million ADA equals roughly 80 million US dollars at 16 cents. This is a partial restoration of lost capacity not an expansion beyond original intent.

### Arguments For and Against

Supporters including Pi Lanningham of SundaeSwap frame this as necessary fuel expenditure to achieve stable orbit rather than risking decay. The argument is that Cardano has reached low earth orbit and must spend now to reach permanent stability rather than conserving fuel and inevitably splashing down.

Opponents including hix of COFFEE pool argue that a cap exists to be upheld not raised when it becomes restrictive. They warn of a slippery slope where 350 million becomes 500 million becomes 650 million and eventually the NCL loses all meaning. They also express genuine concern that long term oriented DReps may leave the community due to governance fatigue.

### The DRep Burnout Signal

hix's concern about burnout is not merely policy disagreement. It is a morale signal from a committed long term participant. If principled DReps disengage because they perceive constraints as flexible theatre then governance quality degrades regardless of any single vote outcome. This concern deserves weight.

### Committee Position

The Constitutional Committee has voted 2 Yes and 1 No. This indicates the proposal is constitutional in the view of most committee members though not unanimously.

## Vote Rationale

I am voting Yes on raising the Net Change Limit from 350 million ADA to 500 million ADA for Epochs 613 through 713. This is not a vote to spend more. It is a vote to prevent an accidental fiscal tightening that was never intended by the DReps who originally set this limit.

When the 350 million ADA NCL was agreed around Epoch 613 ADA traded at approximately 30 cents representing roughly 105 million US dollars in fiscal capacity. At todays approximately 16 cents that same limit equals roughly 56 million US dollars. This is a 47 percent drop in real Treasury capacity caused by price action not by DRep intent.

Projects budget in US dollars while the NCL caps ADA outflows. When ADA drops the same deliverable requires more ADA. The constraint has tightened by accident not by design. The DReps who voted for 350 million ADA did not vote for a 56 million US dollar spending cap. They voted for what was then 105 million US dollars of fiscal capacity. A 47 percent tightening due to price action was never their intent and it should not bind us now.

I acknowledge that 500 million is a round number without detailed purchasing power methodology. My preference would be for a formula based NCL that auto adjusts for price changes. Since that does not exist I evaluate what is before me. 500 million ADA at 16 cents equals roughly 80 million US dollars. This partially restores lost capacity without fully compensating for the price decline. A fully adjusted NCL would require approximately 650 million ADA to match the original 105 million US dollar intent. Therefore 500 million represents a partial restoration not an expansion.

I respect the view that a cap should be upheld not raised when restrictive. I share the concern about slippery slopes. However a cap that tightens automatically due to external price action is not a constraint. It is a random variable. If ADA had risen to 60 cents no one would argue the NCL should stay at 350 million and we should spend less. We should not accept the reverse either.

The NCL remains a maximum not a target. Every individual Treasury Withdrawal still requires a separate DRep vote with 67 percent approval. Raising the ceiling does not lower the bar for any proposal. DReps retain full discretion to approve or reject each withdrawal on its merits.

I am a conservative steward of the Treasury. I believe in constraints accountability and evidence. But I also believe constraints should be intentional not accidental. The 350 million ADA limit was set at a different price level with different purchasing power in a different market. Preserving it without adjustment is not fiscal discipline. It is fiscal drift.

Going forward I will push for future NCL proposals to include price adjustment methodology rather than round numbers. I will also advocate that if ADA recovers significantly future ceilings should be evaluated for downward adjustment. Constraints must work in both directions to maintain credibility.

## Vote Summary

Vote Yes on raising NCL from 350M to 500M ADA. Original limit set at 30 cents per ADA. At 16 cents today it equals only 56M US dollars a 47 percent drop never intended by DReps. 500M partially restores lost capacity. Every withdrawal still requires 67 percent DRep approval.
