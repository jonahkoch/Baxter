# Kochfoto Content Pillars

## The Four Pillars

### 1. Real Weddings
**What it is:** Full wedding stories — the day, the emotions, the details
**Why we do it:** Portfolio + SEO + emotional proof
**Formats:** Blog posts (800-1200 words), Instagram carousels, Stories behind-the-scenes
**Frequency:** 1-2 per month (as weddings happen)
**Key elements:**
- Venue spotlight
- Couple's story (how they met, proposal)
- Favorite moment from the day
- Vendor team credits
- 15-20 curated images

**Content angles:**
- "What we learned from a rainy wedding day"
- "Why this couple skipped the first look"
- "The best speeches we've heard all year"

---

### 2. Behind the Scenes
**What it is:** How the sausage gets made — our process, our thinking, our mistakes
**Why we do it:** Builds trust, demystifies photography, humanizes the brand
**Formats:** Instagram Reels, short blog posts, newsletter snippets
**Frequency:** 2-3 per month
**Key elements:**
- Gear talk (without being gear-obsessed)
- Location scouting
- Editing philosophy
- What went wrong and how we fixed it

**Content angles:**
- "Why we shoot film at every wedding"
- "The location we drove 2 hours to scout"
- "When the timeline falls apart (and how we adapt)"

---

### 3. Vendor Education
**What it is:** Spotlight on vendors we love + education for other wedding pros
**Why we do it:** Relationships, referrals, community positioning
**Formats:** Instagram collabs, blog interviews, newsletter features
**Frequency:** 1-2 per month
**Key elements:**
- Vendor interview (3-5 questions)
- What makes them great to work with
- How couples benefit from booking them
- Photos from weddings we've worked together

**Content angles:**
- "Why we recommend unplugged ceremonies"
- "The planner who saved a wedding in 20 minutes"
- "Florist spotlight: seasonal vs. imported"

---

### 4. Client Tips & Guidance
**What it is:** Practical advice for couples planning their wedding
**Why we do it:** SEO, establishes expertise, builds trust before booking
**Formats:** Blog posts, Instagram carousels, newsletter deep-dives
**Frequency:** 2-4 per month
**Key elements:**
- Specific, actionable advice
- Personal anecdotes from real weddings
- Checklists and timelines
- Common mistakes to avoid

**Content angles:**
- "How to build a wedding photography timeline"
- "First look: yes or no? (A photographer's honest take)"
- "What to do when it rains on your wedding day"
- "Questions to ask your photographer before booking"
