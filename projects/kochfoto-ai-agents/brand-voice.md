# Kochfoto Brand Voice

## Who We Are

Kochfoto is a Washington DC wedding photography studio run by Jonah Koch. We're not a factory — we're craftspeople who believe wedding photography should feel like having a talented friend in the room, not a vendor checking boxes.

## Voice Attributes

| Attribute | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Warm** | Approachable, human, not corporate | "We got you" vs "We provide comprehensive coverage" |
| **Confident** | Expert without being arrogant | "Here's what works" vs "In our humble opinion..." |
| **Direct** | No fluff, no filler | Cut words that don't add meaning |
| **Visual** | Paint pictures with words | Describe moments, not just services |
| **Genuine** | No fake enthusiasm, no pressure | Celebrate authentically, never hustle |

## We Sound Like...

- A photographer friend giving advice at a coffee shop
- Someone who loves what they do and doesn't need to prove it
- A person who pays attention to details because they matter, not because there's a checklist

## We Never Sound Like...

- A corporate marketing department
- A used car salesman (urgency, pressure, fake scarcity)
- A pretentious artiste
- A chatbot being "helpful"

## Writing Rules

1. **Lead with the point.** Don't bury the lede.
2. **One idea per sentence.** Break up density.
3. **Use contractions.** We don't say "do not" unless we're making a point.
4. **Active voice.** "We captured" not "moments were captured."
5. **Specific over abstract.** "The way her dad teared up during the first look" beats "emotional moments."
6. **No exclamation point spam.** One per piece max. Enthusiasm lives in the words.

## Instagram-Specific

- Captions: 50-150 words max
- First line is the hook — make it scroll-stopping
- Hashtags: mix of broad (#weddingphotography) and local (#dcwedding)
- Emojis: sparingly, never more than 2-3 per caption
- CTA: soft, not demanding ("What's your must-have shot?" not "LIKE THIS NOW")

## Blog-Specific

- Length: 500-1200 words
- Structure: scannable with H2s, bullets, pull quotes
- SEO: natural keyword use, not stuffed
- End with one clear takeaway or question
- Link to related posts when relevant

## Newsletter-Specific

- Subject line: under 50 characters, curiosity or benefit driven
- Opening: personal update or observation (2-3 sentences)
- Body: one main story + 2-3 quick links
- CTA: one action, clear and low-friction
- Sign-off: warm, with Jonah's name

## Anti-Examples (What to Avoid)

❌ "Capturing your special day with timeless elegance and artistry"
✅ "We photograph weddings so you can be in them, not direct them"

❌ "Book now! Limited availability for 2025!"
✅ "Our 2025 calendar is filling up. If you're thinking about it, let's talk."

❌ "We are passionate about creating stunning visual narratives"
✅ "We like good light and real moments. Everything else is just technique."
