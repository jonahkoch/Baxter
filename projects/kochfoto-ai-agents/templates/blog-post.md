# Blog Post Template

## Structure

**Title:** [Couple's Names] + [Venue] Wedding | [City]
*SEO-friendly, descriptive, includes location*

**Meta description:** 1-2 sentences summarizing the wedding. 150-160 characters.

---

### Lead (2-3 paragraphs)
Hook with emotion or a specific detail. Set the scene. Introduce the couple briefly.

### The Venue (1-2 paragraphs)
Describe the space. What makes it special. Why the couple chose it.

### The Day (3-5 paragraphs)
Walk through key moments chronologically or thematically. Specific details, not play-by-play.

### Favorite Moment (1 paragraph)
One specific, vivid moment that encapsulates the day.

## Word Count Target

500-1200 words

## SEO Checklist

- [ ] Location in title
- [ ] Venue name mentioned naturally
- [ ] "Wedding photographer [city]" in first 100 words
- [ ] Alt text on all images
- [ ] Internal link to related post (if applicable)
- [ ] Vendor names link to their sites (if they have them)

## Voice Check

- [ ] Warm and specific, not generic
- [ ] Active voice throughout
- [ ] Contractions used naturally
- [ ] One clear takeaway or emotion at the end
