# Instagram Caption Template

## Structure

**Hook (1 line):** Scroll-stopper — emotion, question, or bold statement

**Body (2-4 short paragraphs):** Story, context, or insight

**CTA (1 line):** Soft engagement prompt

**Hashtags:** 8-15 mixed broad + local

---

## Example

**Hook:**
She asked her dad to help with her veil. He ended up holding it together for the next ten minutes.

**Body:**
We talk a lot about the big moments — the first look, the vows, the dance. But sometimes the best stuff happens in the quiet in-between.

Sarah's dad wasn't supposed to be part of getting ready. He was going to wait downstairs. But when he saw her struggling with the veil, he just... stepped in. No one asked. He just knew.

Ten minutes of adjustments, laughter, and one very soft "you look beautiful." We shot the whole thing from the corner and barely breathed.

**CTA:**
What's the quiet moment from your wedding you still think about?

**Hashtags:**
#dcwedding #weddingphotography #fatherofthebride #weddingmoments #dcweddingphotographer #realwedding #behindthescenes

---

## Voice Check

- [ ] Warm, not corporate
- [ ] Specific details, not generic
- [ ] Active voice
- [ ] One idea per sentence
- [ ] Contractions used naturally
- [ ] No exclamation point spam
- [ ] Soft CTA, not demanding

## Length Check

- [ ] 50-150 words
- [ ] Hook fits on first line without truncation
