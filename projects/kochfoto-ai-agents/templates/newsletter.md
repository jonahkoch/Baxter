# Newsletter Template

## Structure

**Subject line:** Under 50 characters. Curiosity or benefit driven.

---

### Opening (2-3 sentences)
Personal update, observation, or something you're thinking about. Not a sales pitch.

---

### Main Story (1-2 paragraphs)
One specific thing from a recent wedding, a client interaction, or something you noticed. Make it feel like a letter, not a broadcast.

---

### Quick Links (2-3 items)
- [Recent blog post] — one sentence why it's worth reading
- [Instagram post] — context on what made this one special
- [Vendor spotlight or resource] — why you're sharing it

---

### Closing (1-2 sentences)
Warm sign-off. Can be a question or an invitation.

**— Jonah**

---

## Example

**Subject:** The photo she didn't know she wanted

---

Hey [First Name],

Last Saturday I was shooting a wedding at [Venue] and something happened that I've been thinking about all week.

During getting ready, the bride's grandmother was sitting in the corner, watching everything. Quiet, smiling, not in anyone's way. I almost didn't shoot it — it was just a woman in a chair. But something made me lift the camera.

Later, when I sent the preview gallery, that was the photo she cried over. Not the dress, not the first look. Her grandmother, watching her become a bride.

The photos you think you want and the photos you actually need are rarely the same thing. That's why we don't work from shot lists.

## Send Schedule

- **Frequency:** 1-2x per month
- **Best days:** Tuesday or Wednesday
- **Best time:** 10am ET

## Voice Check

- [ ] Sounds like a letter from Jonah, not a company
- [ ] One main story, not a roundup
- [ ] Soft CTA, not a sales push
- [ ] Subject line makes people curious
