## Touchpoint Schedule

### Pre-Booking
- Inquiry: Respond within 2 hours
- Consultation: Within 1 week of inquiry
- Proposal: Within 24 hours of consultation
- Follow-up: 3 days after proposal, then 1 week

### Post-Booking
- Welcome packet: Within 48 hours
- Engagement session: 3-6 months before wedding
- Timeline planning: 2 months before
- Final details: 2 weeks before
- Day-of: arrive early, last check-in

### Post-Wedding
- Sneak peek: Within 48 hours
- Full gallery: 6-8 weeks
- Album design: 2 weeks after gallery delivery
- Review request: 1 month after gallery
- Anniversary check-in: 1 year
