## Meeting Agenda

### Consultation Agenda
1. **Connect** (5 min) — How's planning going? Any wins or stress points?
2. **Vision** (10 min) — Walk through Pinterest, discuss priorities, must-haves
3. **Logistics** (5 min) — Timeline questions, vendor team, special considerations
4. **Package** (5 min) — Confirm coverage needs, discuss add-ons (album, second shooter)
5. **Next steps** (5 min) — Proposal timeline, contract, engagement session scheduling

1. **Confirm details** (5 min) — Final headcount, vendor list, shot list requests
2. **Walk through timeline** (15 min) — Photo opportunities, buffer time, sunset calculation
3. **Family formals** (5 min) — Shot list, names, family dynamics to know
4. **Special moments** (5 min) — Surprises, traditions, sensitive situations
5. **Final logistics** (5 min) — Venue restrictions, parking, vendor meals
