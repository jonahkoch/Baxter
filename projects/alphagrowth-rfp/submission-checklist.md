### Field 4: Project, One Line

### Field 5: Why Does Cardano Need This, and Why Build It Inside PRIME?
The Alpha Growth ecosystem audit (Section 2.3, Category E1) scores Cardano's incentive and user-acquisition infrastructure at 28.8/100 — the third-lowest DeFi score. The audit identifies four specific capabilities that are currently impossible: (1) programmatic incentive distribution (all campaigns are manual airdrops), (2) on-chain attribution (no ability to prove which capital came from which campaign), (3) verifiable reward accounting (no audit trail), and (4) reaching non-Cardano holders ("incentive spend defaults to the audience already holding Cardano assets and is liable to recirculate existing liquidity rather than expand it").

PRIME needs this because its $200M TVL target cannot be met by competing for the same ~11,500 active addresses. Without acquisition infrastructure, PRIME-funded protocols will cannibalize each other's user bases. Building inside PRIME means the acquisition engine is co-designed with the protocols that need users, with milestones tied to verifiable net-new wallet activations — not vanity metrics.

Additionally, while Cardano's eUTxO model eliminates predatory MEV spam (Flashbots documents >50% of EVM blockspace is bot-driven), it also hasn't attracted enough healthy automated trading like arbitrage that improves price efficiency. Surge (live since Jan 2026) already runs cross-DEX arbitrage strategies, and open-source CEX-DEX bots are active — but infrastructure gaps (bridging 41.3/100, concentrated liquidity 27.5/100, vault standards 36.3/100) constrain their scale. PRIME-funded infrastructure would unlock more genuine, non-predatory economic activity.
