## Slide 2: The Problem

# Cardano Has Holders. It Doesn't Have Users.

**Alpha Growth Ecosystem Audit — The Activity Crisis**

| Metric | Cardano | Ethereum | Sui |
|--------|---------|----------|-----|
| Active Addresses | **11,527** | 537,223 | 100,738 |
| 24h Chain Fees | **$956** | $196,846 | $1,935 |
| DeFi TVL / Market Cap | **~1.0%** | ~18.1% | ~14.5% |
| Protocols Tracked | **63** | 1,968 | 147 |

**What this means:**
- Cardano has a **$6.85B market cap** but only **$67M in DeFi TVL**
- The vast majority of ADA holders have never interacted with a DeFi protocol
- 63 protocols exist but lack an onboarding layer
- Every new primitive (vaults, CL, ALM, curated lending) requires education before adoption

> PRIME is funding infrastructure. But infrastructure without users is just code.

> **Context on metrics:** Flashbots research documents that MEV bots consume >50% of blockspace on major EVM chains, artificially inflating "active addresses" and volume. Cardano's eUTxO model structurally prevents this — no mempool visibility for reordering, no transaction-level expressivity for on-chain probing. Cardano's 11,527 active addresses represent genuine human decisions, not bot scripts. The engagement gap is real, but the baseline is cleaner.

## Slide 3: The Solution

# A Content and Engagement Engine

**Not a Discord server. A conversion funnel.**

- **Educational Content** — Video + written explainers for every PRIME-funded protocol and primitive
- **"Cardano DeFi 101" Series** — Wallet setup, first swap, first lending deposit, yield strategies
- **On-Chain Challenges** — Users complete tasks (first deposit, first LP, first borrow) and earn NFT badges
- **Live Office Hours** — Monthly Q&A where users ask questions about any PRIME protocol
- **User Feedback Loops** — Qualitative insights from community members fed back to protocol teams

**The outcome:** Holders who understand, trust, and actively use Cardano DeFi.
