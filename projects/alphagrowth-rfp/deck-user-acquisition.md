## Slide 2: The Problem

# Cardano Has DeFi. It Has No Pipeline.

**Alpha Growth Audit — Category E1: Incentive & User Acquisition Infrastructure**

| Metric | Cardano | Ethereum | Sui |
|--------|---------|----------|-----|
| Score | **28.8/100** | 90 | 37.5 |
| Active Addresses | **11,527** | 537,223 | 100,738 |
| 24h Fees | **$956** | $196,846 | $1,935 |

**Four things that are currently impossible:**
1. ❌ Programmatic incentive distribution (all manual)
2. ❌ On-chain attribution (can't prove which campaign drove which deposit)
3. ❌ Verifiable reward accounting (no audit trail)
4. ❌ Reaching non-Cardano holders (incentives recirculate existing liquidity)

> *"Incentive spend defaults to the audience already holding Cardano assets and is liable to recirculate existing liquidity rather than expand it."*
> — Alpha Growth Ecosystem Audit, Section 2.3

**PRIME's $200M TVL target cannot be met by competing for the same 11,500 users.**

> **Context on metrics:** Flashbots research shows MEV bots consume >50% of gas on EVM chains like Base and OP Mainnet, with just 2 entities responsible for >80% of spam. Cardano's eUTxO model eliminates MEV spam structurally — no front-running, no sandwich attacks, no mempool probing. This means Cardano's 11,527 active addresses are genuine users, not bot wallets. The gap with Ethereum is smaller than it appears when you strip out automated activity.

## Slide 3: The Solution

# A Build-and-Operate Acquisition Engine

**Not a marketing agency. Infrastructure.**

- **Campaign Configuration Dashboard** — PRIME protocols design incentive campaigns with rules, caps, and eligibility criteria
- **On-Chain Attribution** — Every deposit traced to its source campaign via referral codes and wallet tagging
- **Automated Reward Distribution** — Users claim rewards based on verified, on-chain qualifying activity
- **Public Reporting** — Transparent dashboards showing spend, attributed TVL, and cost per net-new user
- **Cross-Chain Distribution** — Partnerships with wallets, bridges, and ramps to reach EVM-native users

**The outcome:** Net-new wallets, verifiable on-chain, at a measured cost.

## Slide 4: How It Works

```
[EVM User on Base/Ethereum]
         ↓
[Bridge Partner / Wallet Referral]
         ↓
[Cardano Wallet Setup] ← Guided onboarding content
         ↓
[First DeFi Interaction] ← Tracked via referral code
         ↓
[Attribution Engine] ← Links deposit to specific campaign
         ↓
[Reward Distribution] ← Automated based on verified activity
         ↓
[Public Dashboard] ← PRIME + protocols see what's working
```

**Key insight:** The audit notes only **$11.06M of absorption capacity** before yields compress. This means acquisition must be **targeted and measured** — not spray-and-pray. Our attribution system is built for exactly this constraint.
